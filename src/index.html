About Us

We're a Philippines-based software consultancy built for businesses that need senior-level engineering without the overhead. Whether you're starting from scratch or extending what you already have, we deliver production-ready software at competitive rates.

  • Async-first workflow: timezone-flexible and offshore-ready.
  • Experienced team of engineers, designers, and project managers.
  • Vetted network of developers ready to scale with your project's needs.

Code Lambda was founded on a simple idea: great software doesn’t have to break the budget. We are a small team of IT professionals with over a decade of experience developing, managing, and supporting projects for clients across North America, Europe, and Australia.

We pride ourselves on efficiency. We hate waste as much as you do, and we believe projects thrive when given just the right resources to succeed.

Why choose CodeLambda for your next project?

Let's clear some questions out of the way.

01 Why hire a 🇵🇭 PH based team instead of a local agency?

We offer a very competitive price compared to local agencies. You'd be dealing with us directly, which means no middleman that inflates the costs. A generous quote from a US-based agency could start off with $100/hr + management costs.

Depending on your project requirements, for that price point, we could already provide a Software Engineer + Business Analyst bundle that'll get you through your MVP!

02 How do you handle projects if you're a small team?

Our secret sauce is our ruthless refinement workshops. We take in your ideas, uncover gaps, and work with you intensively to get manageable product requirements. This protects both of us: we get scope creep under control, and you get clear milestones towards your goals.

We don't just send a sales person or a junior developer to these refinement workshops. The team that will work on your project will be closely working with you from idea to MVP. You'd be delightfully surprised how far you can go with a dedicated 3-person team.

03 What does a typical engagement look like?

We operate on clear milestones and deliverables over open-ended retainers. Depending on your requirements, this could mean a 3-person team of a pair of Software Engineers and a Business Analyst over a period of 2-6 months.

Once we got your project operational, we can then discuss how to handle further development & ongoing support. Our team will always be of assistance whichever direction your project proceeds.

Work Process

How does Code Lambda deliver excellent results?

01

Discovery & Scoping

We start by understanding your business, not just your requirements. Before any code is written, we nail down what your project needs, what success looks like, and what it'll cost.

This is where our refinement workshops come in. We make sure that we can break down any requirements into manageable chunks that everyone on the team can understand. We're invested in your success, so we're not afraid to poke holes and identify gaps as we know this is key to a smooth development process.

02

Architecture & Planning

This is where we do just enough to get things rolling. A software project is a dynamic process: we know that adjustments will happen. These adjustments will only manifest when we start doing the work.

We pick up work items and continually integrate everything as part of a core feedback loop. This allows us to continuously learn and improve. Alignment is part of our daily work: you're in the front seat of what's being worked on next. Challenges are accepted and given the spotlight, we make pain points obvious so they can be remedied immediately.

Development & Launch

We continuously integrate and deploy our code from Day 1 -- which enables you to see, test, and give feedback on our daily work. This also makes MVP deployments painless, as we'd have uncovered most surprises way beyond the point of launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a typical project cost?

Every project is unique and is priced individually. But we could guarantee that we offer competitive rates compared to local agencies.

How long does a project take?

For very lean MVPs it typically ranges between 2-4 months. Integration projects typically are shorter. Once we gathered enough requirements, we appropriately size work items and give a detailed estimate based on that.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes — we're async-first and timezone-flexible, which makes us a natural fit for North American, Australian, and European clients.

What happens after the project is delivered?

Handover includes documentation and a walkthrough. If you need ongoing support or additional features after launch, we can discuss a retainer or scope a follow-on project. You own everything: code and server access is given on day one.
